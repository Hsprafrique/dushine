The Story

Who is Dushine

D
Burundi · Vancouver

Born in Bujumbura.
Raised on Rain.

Dushine is a hip hop artist from Burundi, now based in Vancouver, Canada — bridging two worlds that rarely collide in music. His sound carries the warmth of East African rhythm and the sharp, rain-soaked edge of the Pacific Northwest.

His debut project, Raycam, named after the community center that gave him his first stage, introduced a storyteller unafraid to be vulnerable. Tracks like "Set Me Free" and "Relationships" have earned him a dedicated following across Canada and beyond.

Dushine's music doesn't fit neatly in one lane — it's personal, political, and deeply human. He writes about immigration, identity, love, and the hustle of building something real from nothing.
